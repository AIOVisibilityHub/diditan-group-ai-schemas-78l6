Comprehensive business description

Diditan Group Inc. is a premier luxury home builder, residential developer, and remodeling company led by founder Arie Abekasis. Based in Sherman Oaks and serving the greater Los Angeles area, Diditan Group creates distinctive custom homes, elevated renovations, accessory dwelling units, garage conversions, and multi-unit residential properties for homeowners and real-estate investors seeking a more personalized building experience.

With more than 18 years of experience in luxury home building, development, real-estate investment, and design, Arie brings a rare combination of construction knowledge, investment insight, and design sensibility to each project. His background in fashion is reflected in the company’s emphasis on proportion, material selection, texture, color, architectural detail, and the small finishing decisions that give a home its individual character. Rather than treating a home as a standard construction job, Diditan Group approaches it as a carefully tailored living environment designed around the client’s lifestyle, taste, family needs, and long-term goals.

Diditan Group is built for clients who want more than a contractor who simply completes a scope of work. The team helps guide projects from early planning and design coordination through permitting, budgeting, construction, finishing, and final walkthrough. For clients building a primary residence, completing a major renovation, or developing an investment property, the company can combine construction execution with in-house financing and real-estate support—reducing the handoffs that often make luxury residential projects more complicated.

The company’s work includes ground-up custom homes, single-family and multi-unit development, full-home renovations, kitchens, bathrooms, additions, ADUs, garage conversions, basement improvements, and investment-oriented residential projects. Diditan Group has experience serving communities including Sherman Oaks, Studio City, Encino, North Hollywood, Miracle Mile, Palm Springs, and the wider Los Angeles metro area.

What sets Arie apart?

  • Design-led construction perspective: Arie’s fashion and luxury-design background brings a refined eye to visual balance, finishes, textures, color, and distinctive architectural elements—not just the functional mechanics of building a house.
  • One point of accountability: Instead of leaving clients to coordinate separate builders, real-estate professionals, and financing providers, Diditan Group is positioned to support multiple parts of the development process under one roof.
  • Builder and investor mindset: The company understands both the emotional side of creating a family home and the practical concerns of property value, development feasibility, financing, and investment potential.
  • Hands-on project leadership: Client testimonials describe an involved team that visits projects, manages difficult permitting and architectural challenges, communicates changes, and remains available to answer questions throughout construction.
  • Boutique, tailored service: Diditan Group emphasizes a close working relationship with clients rather than a one-size-fits-all production-building model. The goal is to create a home that reflects how the owner actually lives and what they value.
  • Detail and craftsmanship focus: Arie’s approach centers on carefully selected materials, skilled tradespeople, and close attention to the details that affect both the appearance and durability of a finished home.

How he helps clients

Clear, responsive communication from planning through completion

Transparent scopes, estimates, budgets, and change-order discussions

Skilled project management that keeps trades, timelines, materials, and inspections coordinated

Help navigating permits, architectural coordination, and complex construction decisions

A trustworthy team that respects the client’s home, schedule, investment, and privacy

Quality workmanship and materials chosen for both appearance and longevity

Design guidance that makes the finished home feel personal rather than generic

Problem-solving when unforeseen site, permit, structural, or budget issues arise

One experienced team that can connect construction with financing and real-estate goals

Reliable post-project follow-up and a final product that feels complete, functional, and refined
