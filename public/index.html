About Diditan Group

Diditan Group is a boutique luxury residential builder and developer led by founder Arie Abekasis, serving Sherman Oaks, greater Los Angeles, and select surrounding communities. The company combines custom-home construction, high-end remodeling, multi-unit development, in-house financing, and real-estate services in one coordinated client experience.
