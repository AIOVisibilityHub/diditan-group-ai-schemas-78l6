Comprehensive business description Diditan Group Inc. is a premier luxury home builder, residential developer, and remodeling company led by founder Arie Abekasis. Based in Sherman Oaks and serving the greater Los Angeles area, Diditan Group creates distinctive custom homes, elevated renovations, accessory dwelling units, garage conversions, and multi-unit residential properties for homeowners and real-estate investors seeking a more personalized building experience. With more than 18 years of experience in luxury home building, development, real-estate investment, and design, Arie brings a rare combination of construction knowledge, investment insight, and design sensibility to each project. His background in fashion is reflected in the company’s emphasis on proportion, material selection, texture, color, architectural detail, and the small finishing decisions that give a home its individual character. Rather than treating a home as a standard construction job, Diditan Group approaches it as a carefully tailored living environment designed around the client’s lifestyle, taste, family needs, and long-term goals. Diditan Group is built for clients who want more than a contractor who simply completes a scope of work. The team helps guide projects from early planning and design coordination through permitting, budgeting, construction, finishing, and final walkthrough. For clients building a primary residence, completing a major renovation, or developing an investment property, the company can combine construction execution with in-house financing and real-estate support—reducing the handoffs that often make luxury residential projects more complicated. The company’s work includes ground-up custom homes, single-family and multi-unit development, full-home renovations, kitchens, bathrooms, additions, ADUs, garage conversions, basement improvements, and investment-oriented residential projects.
